Active listening, made playful

Build the band one sound at a time.

More Bands is not about scores or endless rewards. It gives children a simple way to notice what changes when one musical part joins—or drops out of—the whole song.

01

Band mode

Tap any musician to switch their part on or off. Long-press one to bring that instrument forward and hear it more clearly.

02

Instruments mode

Drag each instrument to its musician. The band grows as every player finds the right sound.

03

Your own sound

Tap the red circle to record a short sound, and it answers back whenever the empty stage is tapped. It stays on the device, and parents can switch the whole feature off.

04

Keep exploring

Tap animals and objects for playful sounds, then move between genres to discover a completely different musical world.

Privacy first, not privacy later

A toy that knows nothing about your child.

More Bands has no accounts, no profiles, no analytics and no advertising. It was not built to collect anything, so there is nothing for us to store, lose, or sell.

  • No data collection

    No name, no email, no age, no device identifier. Playing More Bands creates no profile anywhere, because there is nowhere for a profile to go.

  • No third-party code

    The app ships with zero advertising, analytics or attribution SDKs. Nothing in it phones home, and there is no hidden partner receiving a copy of your child's play.

  • It works offline

    Every drawing and every sound is already inside the app. Put the device in airplane mode and nothing changes — the only network traffic is Apple's own purchase and restore flow.

  • Recordings never leave

    If your child records a sound for the stage, it is written to this device only. It is excluded from backups and iCloud, and it is deleted the moment a parent turns recording off.

  • Feedback without a server

    The feedback form hands your message to your own mail app. We have no backend to receive it any other way, and you see exactly what is being sent before you send it.

  • Parents hold the switches

    Settings sit behind a parental gate: a daily playing-time limit, recording on or off, stage effects, atmosphere sounds, and the quiet button.
